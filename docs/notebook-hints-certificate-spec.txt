THE MET: HIDDEN TREASURES
Feature Specification — Detective Notebook, Magnifying Glass Hints, Detective Certificate
Version 1.0 | October 22, 2026

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Add three complementary features to the existing mobile-first museum scavenger hunt: (1) a collectible Detective Notebook, (2) progressive Magnifying Glass Hints, and (3) a personalized Detective Certificate. This is an additive implementation specification, not a replacement for the existing v2 technical specification or the onboarding/adventure-lifecycle specification. Preserve all ten approved two-line riddles, gallery/quest assignments, photo-first AI verification, parent/manual fallback, gallery-map routing constraints (including no direct 817–818 edge), privacy protections, avatar onboarding, early ending, and confirmed restart behavior.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- Keep attention on physical artworks, not the screen.
- Reward discovery and curiosity, never speed or competition.
- Use the child's chosen avatar and its predefined name; never request a real name.
- No timer, leaderboard, public profile, or account.
- Maintain an elegant museum-adventure aesthetic (warm ivory, charcoal, muted gold, burgundy/forest-green accents); accessible mobile typography and touch targets.
- Ensure all new features work without audio, and most functionality works offline after initial load.

1. DETECTIVE NOTEBOOK

Concept
The Notebook is a personal, collectible record of treasures discovered during the current adventure. Each successfully verified or grown-up-confirmed quest unlocks one notebook entry. The notebook is available from the main game menu and from the success screen.

Entry content (only for unlocked quests)
- Quest number and gallery number.
- Treasure/detail name.
- Artwork title and artist.
- Artwork thumbnail only if image rights and asset availability permit.
- One brief, kid-friendly fact, sourced or editorially reviewed.
- A unique collectible stamp or emblem (e.g., monocle, horse, fan, elephant, tower, travelers, flower, figure, vase, book), using original graphics.
- Verification indicator: AI-confirmed or grown-up-confirmed; do not rank one above the other.
- Optional link to the official Met artwork page.

Locked entries
- Display an elegant placeholder such as 'Mystery #7 — Not discovered yet' and the gallery number only if that gallery has already been revealed as part of normal game navigation.
- Do not leak the artist, painting title, target object, answer image, or future hint text.

Interactions
- From a successful verification: 'ADD TO MY NOTEBOOK' (the entry is already saved when the quest is marked complete; this CTA opens the notebook, not a second write operation).
- A small unobtrusive Notebook icon/button in game navigation.
- Notebook opens as a scrollable grid/list of ten entries, with a detail view for unlocked treasures.
- Optional subtle page-turn or stamp-unlock animation; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Clearly show count: '{N} treasures discovered'.
- Avoid requiring a child to read long text before continuing to the next gallery.

Persistence
- Notebook is derived from the adventure's completedQuests and canonical quest metadata; do not maintain a conflicting second source of truth.
- Entries survive refresh, app closure, weak signal, and End Adventure Early.
- On confirmed Restart Adventure, clear notebook unlocks along with quest progress, while preserving age/avatar.
- If a quest is temporarily skipped due to closure, keep it locked/marked unavailable, never falsely unlocked.

2. MAGNIFYING GLASS HINTS

Concept
Replace the generic Hint presentation with a tappable magnifying-glass affordance that reveals two progressive hints per quest, with a subtle 'invisible ink' or lens-reveal visual treatment.

UI
- Visible button: 'MAGNIFYING GLASS — GET A HINT' with magnifying-glass icon and accessible label.
- First tap reveals Hint 1; second tap reveals Hint 2.
- Once revealed, hints remain accessible when revisiting that quest, including after refresh.
- Show 'Hint 1 of 2' and 'Hint 2 of 2' to communicate progression.
- After both hints are revealed, the button becomes 'REVIEW HINTS', not a broken or endlessly incrementing action.
- Make the reveal immediate with an optional short animation; no mandatory wait, drag, rubbing gesture, or device motion.

Content
- Reuse the existing two progressive hints for each of the ten quests unless a content editor deliberately changes hint text.
- Never modify the canonical approved two-line riddles.
- Hint 1 should gently narrow the search; Hint 2 may be more specific but should avoid stating the answer outright.
- Hints do not reduce rewards, ranks, certificate eligibility, or the legitimacy of a discovery.

Accessibility and offline
- Use a real button, keyboard operable, minimum 44x44px target, visible focus, adequate contrast.
- The hint text must exist in accessible DOM text, not only in a visual effect.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion; show instantly when enabled.
- No camera, sound, network, or special permissions needed.
- Store hintsUsed[questId] as 0, 1, or 2, clamped and validated.

3. DETECTIVE CERTIFICATE

Concept
Provide a personalized keepsake featuring the child's selected avatar and predefined avatar name. Offer an appropriate certificate for both all-ten completion and early ending, with clearly distinct wording.

Full completion
Heading: 'THE MET: HIDDEN TREASURES'
Award title: 'LEGENDARY ART DETECTIVE'
Suggested text: 'This certifies that {avatarName} discovered all 10 hidden treasures at The Met.'
Include avatar illustration, 10/10 treasures discovered, completion date (optional; local date), and tasteful original decorative border/stamp.

Early ending
Heading: 'THE MET: HIDDEN TREASURES'
Award title: 'EXPLORER OF HIDDEN TREASURES'
Suggested text: '{avatarName} discovered {N} hidden treasures during a museum adventure.'
Show the actual discovered count without a failure message or implying full completion.
- Offer the early-ending certificate even at zero discoveries, but use suitable wording such as 'began a museum adventure' rather than claiming treasures were found.
- Early ending must never issue the 10/10 title.

Access and export
- Show certificate CTA on the full-completion screen and early-ending summary screen.
- Also allow re-opening the certificate from the Notebook or adventure summary.
- Provide 'SAVE AS IMAGE' and 'PRINT' where technically supported; a clean browser print layout is acceptable for MVP.
- Generate the certificate locally in the browser where practical; do not upload personal data or require an account.
- Use original avatar and decorative assets. Do not imply the certificate is officially issued or endorsed by The Metropolitan Museum of Art; label it as a game keepsake and avoid unauthorized Met logos/seals.
- Include a small note such as 'A Hidden Treasures adventure keepsake — not an official museum certificate.'
- Do not collect or print the child's real name.

Behavior on resumption/restart
- An early-ending certificate reflects the count at the time of ending. If the adventure resumes, the app may show a refreshed current-progress certificate; do not misrepresent it as a full-completion award.
- On confirmed restart, prior adventure certificate state is cleared with progress. If a parent saved/printed an image externally, the app cannot delete that external copy.

4. DATA MODEL / INTEGRATION

Existing source of truth:

interface AdventureState {
  status: 'not_started' | 'in_progress' | 'ended_early' | 'completed';
  currentQuest: number;
  completedQuests: number[];
  hintsUsed: Record<number, number>;
  attempts: Record<number, number>;
  verificationMethod: Record<number, 'ai-photo' | 'grown-up' | 'manual'>;
  startedAt?: string;
  endedAt?: string;
  completedAt?: string;
}

interface PlayerProfile {
  age: 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | '12+';
  avatarId: string;
  onboardingComplete: boolean;
}

Optional metadata:

interface NotebookPresentation {
  questId: number;
  stampAsset: string;
  shortFact: string;
  artworkImageUrl?: string;
  metObjectUrl?: string;
}

Derived state:
- notebookUnlocked(questId) = completedQuests.includes(questId)
- treasureCount = completedQuests.length
- fullCertificateEligible = status === 'completed' && treasureCount === 10
- earlyCertificateEligible = status === 'ended_early'

Important: canonical quest IDs are not gallery numbers; keep this distinction explicit. Do not expose unsolved answers through notebook metadata on pre-solve screens. A determined user can inspect client assets, so do not claim client-side answer secrecy is cryptographic; server-side verification targets remain server-side as already specified.

5. USER FLOWS

A. Find a treasure:
Quest riddle -> magnifying-glass hint (optional) -> photograph -> AI or grown-up verification -> success -> notebook entry unlocks -> 'ADD TO MY NOTEBOOK' or 'SHOW ME THE WAY' -> next gallery.

B. Revisit:
Open Notebook -> view unlocked entries and locked placeholders -> tap a completed entry -> artwork/detail and short fact -> return to hunt.

C. Finish all ten:
10th discovery -> full completion screen -> 'VIEW DETECTIVE CERTIFICATE' -> save/print or return to Notebook.

D. End early:
Menu -> End Adventure -> confirmation -> early-ending summary -> 'VIEW MY CERTIFICATE' and Notebook -> later resume if desired.

E. Restart:
Menu -> Restart Adventure -> explicit destructive confirmation -> clear quest progress, notebook unlocks, and certificate eligibility -> preserve avatar/age -> start screen.

6. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

1. Each completed quest unlocks exactly one Notebook entry; no duplicates.
2. Unsolved entries do not reveal target art or answers.
3. Notebook progress persists across refresh and End Adventure Early.
4. Confirmed restart clears unlocked entries, but canceled restart changes nothing.
5. Each quest supports exactly two progressive hints via magnifying-glass UI.
6. Hints persist and do not affect rewards.
7. Hint interaction works with touch, keyboard, and reduced-motion settings.
8. Full certificate is available only for all ten completed quests.
9. Early-ending certificate uses truthful partial-progress wording, including at zero discoveries.
10. Certificate uses the predefined avatar name; never requests a real name.
11. Certificate can be viewed and saved/printed where supported, with accessible fallback.
12. Certificate is clearly a game keepsake, not an official Met-issued award.
13. All new content functions on iPhone Safari and Android Chrome.
14. Core Notebook/hints/certificate viewing works offline after assets are cached, where technically feasible.
15. Existing quests, photo verification, gallery routing/closures, onboarding, privacy, early-end, and confirmed-restart behavior remain intact.

7. QA CHECKLIST

- First treasure; repeated verification of same treasure; all ten treasures.
- Open Notebook with 0, 1, 6, and 10 treasures.
- Verify locked cards reveal no future answers.
- Reveal first hint, refresh, reveal second hint, revisit; no third hint.
- Test keyboard/screen-reader/reduced-motion hint experience.
- End adventure at 0 and 6 discoveries; inspect truthful certificate.
- Complete all ten; inspect full award and export.
- Resume an early-ended adventure and then complete it.
- Cancel restart; confirm restart; verify age/avatar remain.
- Offline revisit of cached Notebook and certificate.
- Export/print on iPhone Safari and Android Chrome; use fallback if unsupported.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
Implement as a feature addendum to the existing Hidden Treasures codebase. Do not change the canonical ten riddles, the photo-first answer mechanism, the verified gallery-routing architecture, or the established adventure-state semantics.
